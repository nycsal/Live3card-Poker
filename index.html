How it plays

Everyone gets three cards, and so does Kaka. Place an Ante, look at your hand, then Play (a bet equal to your Ante) or Fold. A fresh deck is shuffled every hand.

Against the dealer

The dealer qualifies with Queen high or better. If the dealer does not qualify, your Ante pays 1 to 1 and your Play bet is returned. If the dealer qualifies, the better hand wins both bets, and a tie pushes.

Hand order

Straight flush, three of a kind, straight, flush, pair, high card. Ace-2-3 is the lowest straight and Ace-King-Queen is the highest.

Ante Bonus

Paid on your hand whether or not you beat the dealer. Lost if you fold.
